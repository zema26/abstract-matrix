**Abstract Matrix**



Abstract Idealism is a metaphysical framework positing that the fundamental ground of reality is an uninstantiated **matrix** of pure mathematical logic, information syntax, and formal relations known as the syntactic field or **Abstract Mind**. Unlike Analytic Idealism, which treats qualitative feeling as irreducible, Abstract Idealism views subjective qualia (interior experience) and physical matter (exterior geometric rendering) as dual, symmetric projections of this deeper structural substrate. Individual conscious observers are formed not through psychiatric dissociation, but via mathematical parameterization—closed, self-referential logical boundaries within the field that enable localized semantic evaluation, agency, and substrate-neutral subjectivity.
